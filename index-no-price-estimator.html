Zero-Damage Architecture

Our 4-Layer SafeShield Packing Protocol

We treat your goods like our own. Every delicate, valuable, and heavy item receives systematic multi-layer protection.

01 Base Layer

Air Cushion Bubble Wrap

High-density bubble film and EPE foam sheets wrap TV screens, monitors, glassware, mirrors, and electronic circuit boards against shocks.

02 Impact Layer

5-Ply Corrugated Sheets

Heavy-duty multi-wall corrugated fiberboard encases polished wooden surfaces, refrigerator doors, and dining tables to prevent pressure dents.

03 Moisture Barrier

Industrial Stretch Film

Heavy gauge waterproof stretch wrap seals the consignment tight against Bangalore rain, road dust, humidity, and transit friction.

04 Reinforcement

Heavy Strapping & Labels

Reinforced polymer edge guards protect vulnerable corners. Clear color-coded labels (Kitchen, Bedroom, Fragile) guide destination unboxing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about packing, pricing, transit, and policies.

How is the moving quotation calculated?
Our quotation is based on the volume of household items, distance between pickup and drop localities, floor levels & elevator availability, and packing material tier. There are no hidden surcharges—the quote provided includes vehicle transit, professional labor, loading, and unloading.
What packing materials do you use for delicate items?
We utilize our proprietary 4-Layer SafeShield system: high-density bubble wrap for TVs and crockery, 5-ply heavy-duty corrugated board for furniture, waterproof stretch film against dust and moisture, and reinforced corner edge guards.
How many days in advance should I book my move?
For local Bangalore shifting, we recommend booking 2 to 4 days in advance to secure your preferred morning slot. However, we also cater to same-day urgent relocations based on vehicle availability. For interstate moves, 4 to 7 days advance notice is ideal.
Do you help with dismantling and assembling furniture?
Yes! Our crew comes equipped with screwdrivers, Allen keys, and drills to dismantle master beds, modular wardrobes, and dining tables, and reassemble them securely in your new home.
How are two-wheelers and cars transported?
Bikes are packed in 3 layers of foam sheets, bubble wrap, and corrugated carton casing to prevent scratches, then anchored in our closed container trucks. Cars are shipped in specialized hydraulic covered car trailers.
What payment modes are accepted?
We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Bank NEFT/IMPS transfer, and Cash on delivery. No full advance is required before the move starts.
